ALPR Week of Action

Published on 2026-07-08

Surveillance Tech Teach-in

Hello Joplin!

We have been quiet for a little bit, but we have been working hard and have things to show for it. August 16-22 in a national ALPR week of action.

We will be participating by hosting a Tech Teach in event at the Joplin Library. There has been a lot of new interest and coverage on surveillance recently. We will:

  • Demystify some of the things you might've recently heard about Flock, Leonardo SignalTrace, and surveillance in general.
  • Talk about Flock detection methods (homegrown in Joplin!)
  • Explain cybersecurity problems with the cameras and why it's not just Flock who should care
  • Explain how to protect yourself from encroaching surveillance

    • For the technical aspects, we will aim the discussion at practical implications everyone can understand rather than technical details for a technical audience.

    The event will be held at the Joplin Public Library in Conference room 1 on August 16, 2026 at 3:00 PM. If you have specific questions you would like to see covered, please send them to our email: deflockjoplintoday@proton.me
